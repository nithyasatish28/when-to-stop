The One-Way River

Your boat drifts past a row of islands, one at a time. Each island has a chest of coins. Land on one island and your voyage ends there. The current is too strong to paddle back.

You win only if you land on the richest island of the whole river. Coin amounts change wildly between voyages, so you can only judge a chest against the ones you've already seen.

After every voyage, you'll see what the 37% rule would have done on the same river.
