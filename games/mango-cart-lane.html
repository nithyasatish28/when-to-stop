Mango Cart Lane

How to play

You're walking down a one-way lane of mango carts. Swipe right to buy the mango, or left to keep walking. You can't go back, and you can buy only one.

You win only if you buy the sweetest mango in the lane. Scores are spread evenly, so a mango scoring 80 is sweeter than 80% of all mangoes anywhere.

How the bar plan works

Buy a mango only if it's the sweetest you've seen so far and its score clears a bar. The bar depends on how many carts are still ahead. With many carts left, only a near-perfect mango is worth it. As the lane runs out, the bar drops.
