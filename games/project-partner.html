How to play

It's project week. You meet your classmates one at a time. Swipe right to ask them to team up, or left to keep looking. Once you move on, you can't go back to someone.

Each classmate has a fit score. But the scoring changes wildly every round: in one class, scores might run to 400, in the next to 90,000. So a score only tells you how someone compares with the people you've already met.

You win if you team up with the best partner in the whole class.
